About Machon Shoshanat Yerushalayim

Machon Shoshanat Yerushalayim is a premier Chabad educational institution dedicated to empowering young women through comprehensive Torah study, personal growth, and Hasidic values in the heart of Jerusalem.

Our program combines rigorous academic standards with a warm, supportive community environment that fosters lifelong friendships and spiritual development.

In-Depth Study

Comprehensive Chasidus and Halacha curriculum taught by experienced educators.

Jerusalem Experience

Immersive Jewish living and inspirational atmosphere in the holy city of Jerusalem.

Personal Growth

Individualized guidance and mentorship tailored to every student's personal journey.
